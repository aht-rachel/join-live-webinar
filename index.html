A Quick Note About Check-in

Please read before joining

When you join the presentation, you'll be asked to complete a short participant survey. This simply helps us get to know our audience and understand who we're presenting to.

Some questions may include your total household income and residency/citizenship. For household income, please consider the combined income of everyone in your household, including yourself, your spouse/partner, or other contributing family members (ideally $50,000/year).

The survey does not affect your ability to join the presentation. Everyone is welcome to attend!
